About our little shop

Why dinosaurs?

We think the world could use more soft things and more silly friends.

A small idea with a big hug

Soft Saur is a pretend little shop for fluffy dinosaur friends. Fern, Peaches, and Moon each have their own personality, but they all have one thing in common: they are ready for a cuddle.

We made this site to share our dino pals and make shopping feel easy. Pick a favorite, have a look around, and say hello if you have a question.
